name: Theme intent
description: User-selected base theme has precedence over verdict moods.
---
Dark is a persistent user choice: verdicts must never turn a Dark or System-dark screen light. Light FALSE is the explicit exception that keeps the dramatic dark treatment.

**Why:** The user reported Dark flipping to white and explicitly requested that verdict moods only tint the cards and decorations in Dark. The revised brand is flat lime-and-ink, not purple or glow-based.

**How to apply:** Preserve base-theme ownership when adding screens or verdict effects. Keep verdict meaning separate from the signature accent, and do not reintroduce gradients or glass effects.
